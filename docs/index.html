Music Bot & Dashboard

Free, open-source Discord music bot and real-time YouTube Music style web dashboard unified into a single lightweight container.

Get Started Docker Compose 37 Commands Accessibility Localization Changelog GitHub

YouTube Music Dashboard

60fps progress interpolation, AMOLED dark mode, 10 dynamic accent themes, Up Next / Lyrics / Related tabs, and mobile PWA navigation.

Timeline Queue & Drag-and-Drop

Browse history, current song, manual queue, and Autoplay suggestions in one timeline. Move even the active track without stopping audio.

7-Mood Autoplay & Synced Lyrics

Radio recommendations from your history and liked tracks, plus synced LRC/ELRC lyrics with click-to-seek and auto-scroll.

Smart Voice Channel Awareness

Automatically switches your active dashboard server to the VC you join, pauses when VC empties, and resumes playback when someone returns.

4-Tier Role Access Control

Cascading Discord permissions across User, DJ, Admin, and Server/Bot Owner tiers, plus live public domain management under System Settings.

Single-Process & SQLite

No MongoDB or extra config files needed. Pre-built GHCR images include bundled Lavalink v4 plugins with ~7-second cold starts.
